Shut the BoxClassroom review edition

Ask a question. When a team answers correctly, they roll the dice and shut tiles that add up to the roll. Every tile shut scores points, and shutting the whole box earns a bonus. Wrong answer? Skip to the next team.

  • After a correct answer, click Roll the dice (or press Space).
  • Click tiles that add up to the roll, then click Shut them (Enter). If no tiles can make the roll, the turn scores 0.
  • Each team has its own box. Shut every tile for the bonus, and the box opens up again.
