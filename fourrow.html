Four in a Row

Four in a RowClassroom review edition

The board starts with 100 black squares. When a team answers correctly, they claim a square, it turns their color and its hidden points are revealed. Spot 4 in a row of one color and draw a line through them for a big bonus. Wrong answer? Skip to the next team.

Rounds
  • After a correct answer, the team clicks any black square. It flips to their color and shows the points inside.
  • The game never points out a 4 in a row. When students spot 4 squares of one color in a line, they draw the line: press on one end square and drag to the other end. A correct line earns that team the bonus, and the game keeps going.
  • Click End game when you are done; the board can fill up completely.
