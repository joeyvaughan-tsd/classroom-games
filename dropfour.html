Drop Four

Drop FourClassroom review edition

Red against Yellow on the classic upright board. When a team answers correctly, they drop a disc into a column. Wrong answer? Skip their turn. Get 4 in a row, then draw a line through them to win the game.

Rounds
  • After a correct answer, the team picks a column (move over it, or use ← →) and clicks or presses Space to drop the disc.
  • Wrong answer? Click Wrong answer, skip (S) and the other team is up.
  • 4 in a row (across, up and down, or diagonal)? Students must draw the line: press on one end disc and drag to the other end. A correct line wins the game, and a new board starts. Press H to show them where if they are stuck.
