Sink the FleetClassroom review edition

Hidden ships lurk on the grid. When a team answers correctly, they fire one shot. A hit bursts into flames, and sinking a whole ship earns a bonus. Play two teams with two fleets, or all teams against one hidden fleet. Wrong answer? Skip to the next team.

Rounds
  • After a correct answer, the team clicks a square on the glowing target grid to fire. Squares already fired at cannot be picked again.
  • A white peg is a miss; a flame is a hit. When every square of a ship is hit, the whole ship appears and is crossed off the fleet list.
  • Two teams, two fleets: the first team to sink the other team's whole fleet wins (+50). All teams vs. one hidden fleet: the game ends when the last ship sinks.
