Advance!

Every team starts on the same gold square. Ask a question; each team that answers correctly moves forward one square. The first team to advance all the way across wins.

Squares to cross
  1. Ask a question. Every team can answer (whiteboards work well).
  2. Click each team that answered correctly, or press its number key. Their next square starts to glow.
  3. Click Advance! (or press Enter) and every marked team moves forward one square.
  4. The first team across the finish line sets off the celebration. Keep playing for 2nd and 3rd place, or start a new game.
