Two teams, one board. When a team answers correctly, they place their piece. Three in a row wins the game. Wrong answer? Skip their turn. Choose your pieces: X and O, milk and cookies, sun and moon, cats and dogs, the TSD seal and the Rangers logo, or upload your own pictures.

  • Wrong answer? Click Wrong answer, skip (S) and the other team is up.
  • Three in a row wins: the line lights up and the winner gets the points. A tie or a win starts a new board automatically.
